4-CIRCUIT PROTECTION VALVE

  • SKU: TM-0045-4-CIRCUIT-PROTECTION-VALVE

4-Circuit Protection Valve for heavy-duty truck pneumatic brake systems. Distributes compressed air to four separate circuits and helps protect unaffected circuits if another circuit loses pressure. Features: Four-circuit air distribution; circuit pressure protection; multiple threaded air connections; black upper housing and metallic lower body. Compatibility: Confirm the original OE number, port sizes, opening/closing pressures and vehicle application before purchase. Similar-looking valves may not be interchangeable.
